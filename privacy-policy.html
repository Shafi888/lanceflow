Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

LanceFlow ("we", "our", or "the app") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled when using our mobile and web application.

1. Data Collection & Local Storage

LanceFlow is designed with a privacy-first, offline-ready architecture:

  • All client records, invoice details, time logs, and business profile information are stored locally on your device using browser LocalStorage.
  • We do not transmit, sell, or sync your financial or invoice data to external third-party advertising servers.

2. App Permissions

LanceFlow requests minimal device permissions necessary for basic app functionality:

  • Storage / Downloads: Required only when you export PDF invoices or download backup JSON files to your device storage.

3. Third-Party Services

LanceFlow does not use third-party tracking cookies or ad networks. Any payment links or external banking details included in your invoices are managed directly between you and your clients.

4. Data Backup & Deletion

Because your data resides on your device, you have complete ownership. You can export your data at any time via the Backup settings, or delete all records instantly by clearing app data or browser cache.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this policy, please contact our support team at support@lanceflow.app.
